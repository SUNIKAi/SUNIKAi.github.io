Why work with Sunikai

Present at both ends

A team in Asia, in daily contact with the mills, and a commercial contact in France. Nobody plays telephone between you and the production line.

Quality control is included

Every container is inspected in the mill before loading, with a photo report. That is the only moment when a problem can be fixed without costing you money.

A delivered price, not an ex-works price

Our quotations are DAP: the figure you receive is the goods unloaded at your place. You can build your margin on it straight away.

PEFC / FSC certifications

Certified supply chains available on our species, to meet your customers' requirements and European timber regulation.

Plantation species

Rubberwood at the end of its latex life, plantation acacia and short-cycle bamboo: renewable resources, not primary forest timber.

Bespoke, not a catalogue

The published dimensions are our standards. A different format, grade or finish? That is precisely what sourcing is for.
