sourcing · import · export

Our services

Three trades, one chain: we find the right product at the right price, we check that it complies before it leaves the mill, and we deliver it to your company.

01

Tailored sourcing

You send us your specification: species, dimensions, grade, annual volume and target budget. We then identify the mill able to produce it under those conditions, and submit samples to you before any commitment.

Our presence in Asia makes the difference. We visit the production sites, assess real capacity and actual lead times, and know which mill to entrust with each type of work.

Is your project outside our standard range? That is precisely our business: we develop the product with you, from the first sample through to production.

  • Partner mill research and audit
  • Samples and approval before ordering
  • Price and lead time negotiation
  • Custom product development

02

Quality control

This is the core of the job. A non-compliant container discovered on arrival in Europe means a dispute 10,000 km away, weeks of discussion and goods you cannot sell.

So we inspect before loading, inside the mill: dimensions, moisture content, glue-line quality, visual sorting, packing and marking. You receive a detailed photo report and you approve — or not — the departure.

This step is included in our prices. It is not an option; it is the reason our clients work with us rather than direct.

  • Pre-shipment inspection inside the mill
  • Detailed photo report
  • Dimension and moisture checks
  • Packing and marking verification

03

Import and DAP delivery

DAP — Delivered At Place — means the price we quote is the price of the goods delivered to you. No freight surprises, no port charges, no forwarder to manage.

We take care of the ocean booking, export documentation, customs clearance and final road haulage. You only settle import duties and taxes, which remain yours under DAP.

In practice: one price, one date, one truck at your loading bay.

  • One single price, delivered to your warehouse
  • Ocean booking and documentation
  • Customs formalities
  • Shipment tracking through to delivery
