Rubberwood Flooring

AB grade · 2 or 4 strips

A light, calm floor in solid rubberwood, in 2 or 4 strips and three finishes. Whitewash, bleached and matt, brings a bright Scandinavian touch.

The 2-strip format reads wider and more contemporary, the 4-strip a finer, more regular pattern. Tongue and groove on all 4 sides, micro-bevel, factory UV lacquer.

Three finishes: Natural for rubberwood's blond tone, Whitewash for bright interiors, Oak to warm the wood towards a golden oak tone.

AB grade, smooth and even surface. Give us the total area and installation method with your enquiry: they determine the size and the quantity to order.

Applications

  • Residential floors
  • Hotel rooms
  • Offices
  • Moderate-traffic retail
