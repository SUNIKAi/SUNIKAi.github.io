Rubberwood Flooring

AB grade · 2 or 4 strips · Natural, Whitewash, Oak

A light, quiet floor available in three finishes. The 2-strip format reads wider and more contemporary; the 4-strip gives a finer, more regular pattern.

The Natural finish keeps rubberwood's pale blond tone. Whitewash lightens and mattes the surface, ideal for bright interiors. Oak warms the wood towards a golden oak tone.

Three thicknesses are available depending on the installation: thinner boards for glue-down on screed, thicker ones when you need sanding allowance or a joist installation.

Give us the total area and the installation method with your enquiry: they drive the thickness, the format and the offcut allowance.

Applications

  • Residential floors
  • Hotel rooms
  • Offices
  • Moderate-traffic commercial spaces
