Caramel Vertical Bamboo Panel

3- or 5-ply · Caramel tone

The same vertical panel as the natural version, heat-treated: the heat caramelises the bamboo and gives it a deep honey tone right through its thickness.

The caramel tone is not a surface stain: it runs through the strip. Sanding or machining an edge keeps the same colour, which makes installation much simpler.

Fine, regular vertical grain, cross-laminated plies for stability: 3-ply in 20, 25 and 30 mm, 5-ply in 40 mm. Available in 2440 × 1220 mm and, in 25 mm, in the 2400 × 650 mm worktop size.

Fast-growing material, FSC certified.

Applications

  • Worktops
  • High-end furniture
  • Wall linings
  • Counters and fit-out
