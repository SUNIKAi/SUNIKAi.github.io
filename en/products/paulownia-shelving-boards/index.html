Paulownia weighs about 300 kg/m³, roughly half as much as most fit-out timbers. A 2 m shelf can be carried in one hand.

AB grade, full-length, sanded and natural in colour: the board is ready to oil or varnish. Controlled drying to limit warping.

Four widths from 300 to 600 mm and two thicknesses: 20 mm for loaded shelves, 18 mm for linings and light furniture.

Applications

  • Shelves and racking
  • Interior furniture
  • Linings
  • Retail fit-out
