The tongue and groove profile on all 4 sides allows continuous jointing with no visible nails, including at board ends.

Two finishes: Natural, in a light wood tone, and Brushed, for a textured look that brings out the grain. Ready to oil, lacquer or paint.

Ultra-fast-growing paulownia, a low-carbon material. About 300 kg/m³: one of the lightest building timbers.

Applications

  • Wall linings
  • Ceilings
  • Interior panelling
  • Loft conversions
