Dark Bamboo Decking

Strand woven · Dark tone

The same board as the Light version, in a dark brown tone obtained through a more intense heat treatment. A look close to dense tropical hardwoods.

The dark tone runs through the board: a scratch or a cut does not reveal pale wood underneath, unlike a surface stain.

In full sun, a dark board heats up more than a light one. Worth considering for a pool surround or a south-facing barefoot terrace.

Light and Dark share the same profile and fixings, so they can be combined on the same project to create banded patterns.

Applications

  • Residential decks
  • Pool surrounds
  • Restaurant terraces
  • Landscaping
