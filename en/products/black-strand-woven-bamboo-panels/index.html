Black Strand Woven Bamboo Panel

Strand woven · Black tone

The top of the bamboo family: fibres are shredded then compressed under very high pressure. The result is a markedly denser material with a dark tone and a fibrous grain.

Strand woven bamboo no longer looks like laminated bamboo: the grain becomes fibrous, almost mineral, with dark highlights. This is the product to pick when you want a deep black in solid wood rather than a laminate.

Densification gives a surface hardness far above standard laminated bamboo, which opens up high-traffic applications.

One machining note: the high density wears tooling faster. Plan for carbide tooling and lighter passes.

Applications

  • Premium worktops
  • Counters and retail fit-out
  • Stair treads
  • Dark wall linings
