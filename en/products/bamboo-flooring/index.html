The horizontal grain shows the bamboo nodes across the full width of each plank: a warm, recognisable pattern.

Two sizes: the 1800 mm wide plank for a contemporary look in open spaces, and the more classic 1020 mm plank. Natural or caramel tone.

Floating click installation, no glue, is quick and clean, including in renovation. Fast-growing material, FSC certified.

Applications

  • Living spaces
  • Open-plan areas
  • Retail
  • Renovation
