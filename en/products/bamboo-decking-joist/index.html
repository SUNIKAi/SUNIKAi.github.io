Strand woven, dark carbonised bamboo: a strong, stable joist, resistant to moisture and wood-boring insects.

40 × 40 mm section, 2100 mm long. Shipped with the decking in the same container, along with the fixing clips.

Using a substructure in the same material as the boards avoids differences in behaviour between joist and deck over time.

Applications

  • Deck substructure
  • Pool surrounds
  • Balconies
  • Boardwalks
