Rubberwood comes from the rubber tree. The timber is harvested at the end of the plantation's productive life, once latex tapping is over: it is a plantation resource, not primary forest, which is exactly why it is available and affordable.

In AB grade, one face is selected to stay visible: even colour, sound and infrequent knots. The back face allows more variation. Panels are shipped sanded, ready for varnish, oil or lacquer.

We can load several thicknesses in the same container, so worktops, shelving and shopfitting structures can be covered by a single order.

Applications

  • Worktops and shelving
  • Shopfitting and furniture
  • Stair treads and risers
  • Interior lining panels
