Bamboo Decking

Strand woven · Light or Dark

A strand woven bamboo decking board, denser and harder than classic tropical hardwoods, in two tones and several profiles. A durable alternative to tropical timber and composite.

  • WOCA pre-oiled
  • R10 anti-slip

Bamboo fibres are carbonised, resin-impregnated then hot-pressed under high pressure. The result is a material with a density of 1230 kg/m³ and a Brinell hardness of 129 kg/mm², higher than ipe.

Two tones: Light, carbonised at 140 °C to keep a golden brown, and Dark, carbonised at 200-220 °C for a deep chocolate brown. Both are factory pre-oiled with WOCA oil and R10 anti-slip.

Two fixing systems: the 139 mm reversible board, end-matched, with hidden stainless steel clips, or the 118 mm cambered board with built-in GRAD fixing. Fire rating Bfl-s1, d0 without chemical retardant, FSC certified and SGS tested.

Applications

  • Residential decks
  • Pool surrounds
  • Restaurant terraces
  • Balconies and boardwalks
