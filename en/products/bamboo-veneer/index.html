The veneer reproduces exactly the two panel patterns — wide-strip horizontal and fine-stripe vertical — and both tones, natural and caramel. It lets you match veneered fronts to solid bamboo edges or tops.

It bonds to MDF, plywood or particleboard with standard woodworking adhesive, in a press or a vacuum bag. Like any thin veneer, it needs a perfectly flat substrate.

The 2460 × 430 mm format covers standard kitchen and wardrobe front heights with no horizontal join.

Applications

  • Furniture fronts
  • Retail fit-out
  • Doors and partitions
  • Yacht and vehicle interiors
