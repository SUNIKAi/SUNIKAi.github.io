The vertical panel is made by pressing fine bamboo strips on edge. The result: a fine, regular stripe, a uniform colour and a structure visible on the edge.

Cross-laminated plies, 3-ply in 20 and 30 mm, 5-ply in 40 mm, keep large surfaces stable and allow edge machining, especially for worktops.

Bamboo reaches maturity in 4 to 5 years and regrows after cutting: a fast-growing material, FSC certified.

Applications

  • Worktops
  • Joinery and furniture
  • Wall linings
  • Interior fit-out
