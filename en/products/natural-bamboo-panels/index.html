Natural Bamboo Panel

Horizontal or vertical · Natural tone

Bamboo in its original pale blond tone. Two possible patterns depending on the press: wide visible strips in horizontal, fine regular stripes in vertical.

Moso bamboo is not a tree but a grass: it reaches full size in a few years and regrows from the stump after cutting. That short cycle is a strong argument with clients who watch their material footprint.

The horizontal panel shows the bamboo nodes across the full width of each strip — a bold, instantly recognisable pattern. The vertical panel, pressed on edge, gives a fine, regular stripe that reads much quieter.

The cross-laminated 3-ply construction keeps thick panels stable and allows edge machining, which matters for worktops.

Applications

  • Worktops
  • Furniture and shopfitting
  • Wall linings
  • Stair treads
